30-Second Elevator

Py.rouette compiles elite embodied decision logic into auditable finite-state decision primitives for AI, robotics, and live decision systems. We formalized an FSM governance protocol (Administrative Execution Protocol v1.0) and deliver scoring modules that improve gating, hysteresis, and risk-decay behavior in production loops.

Problem

Operational decision systems break under noisy inputs because “when to act / when not to act” primitives are often non-auditable, fragile, and not engineered for hysteresis/decay. This causes false triggers, oscillation, and inconsistent stress latency.

Solution

  • Explicit States: named action and state nodes.
  • Guards & Permission Gates: sensor thresholds and decision envelopes.
  • Risk-Decay Primitives: timed exits + hysteresis to prevent flip-flop.
  • Receipts: per-decision audit packets (timestamp, sensor snapshot, rationale).
