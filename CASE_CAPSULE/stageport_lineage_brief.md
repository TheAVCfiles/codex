# StagePort Command-Center Brief (Draft)

## 1) One-line definition

**StagePort is a safety and credential infrastructure that translates dance studio leadership into verified institutional trust signals.**

## 2) Core position

StagePort is **instrumentation, not replacement** for ballet lineage.

Primary preservation chain:

`embodied technique -> measurable signal -> documented score -> preserved lineage`

## 3) Problem statement

Current conditions create a structural gap:

- young dancers can leave training pathways when environments feel unsafe or unstable
- assistant/leadership labor is often not translated into recognized credentials
- institutional systems cannot easily read informal studio stewardship signals
- motion-capture and generative pipelines can extract movement geometry without lineage attribution

## 4) Solution frame

StagePort provides a bridge between studio craft and institutional recognition through three coordinated layers:

1. **Safety + environment layer** (room awareness and stewardship logging)
2. **Credential layer** (verified participation, mentoring, and responsibility records)
3. **Lineage layer** (teacher-student-stager contribution mapping)

Bridge logic:

`studio leadership -> verified stewardship actions -> StagePort record -> university/community recognition`

## 5) Why this scales beyond dance

Dance studios already train transferable behaviors:

- situational awareness
- disciplined attention
- peer mentorship
- spatial accountability

StagePort translates those behaviors into legible signals for schools, campuses, and community systems.

## 6) Pilot specification (factual template)

StagePort Phase 1 Pilot (target template):

- **Objective:** integrate behavioral awareness metrics with credential records
- **Installation context:** university safety/community environment
- **Phase 1 budget:** $2,500
- **Phase 2 target:** $7,500
- **Prototype component:** servo-based environmental signaling loop
- **Development cycle:** 60 days

## 7) Proof timeline format

Use a timeline-first evidence display:

- **2025:** concept and architecture development
- **2026 Q1:** university safety install pilot
- **2026 Q1:** Phase 1 funding secured ($2.5k)
- **2026 Q2:** servo prototype deployment
- **2026 Q2:** Phase 2 funding target ($7.5k)

## 8) Governance and integrity constraints

StagePort must preserve all three at once:

- lineage integrity
- economic survival
- personal autonomy

System guardrails:

- authority remains with teachers, repetiteurs, and repertory stewards
- attribution and transmission records are mandatory
- platform claims must stay evidence-backed and non-speculative

## 9) Positioning guardrail

Do **not** position StagePort as a generic dance app or studio management tool.

Position it as:

**environmental awareness + leadership credential infrastructure**

with dance as the initial proving ground.

## 10) Institutional expansion path

`StagePort vision -> safety/environment install -> institutional validation -> credential recognition -> platform expansion`
