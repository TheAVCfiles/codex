Py.rouette — Decision Gating for Noisy Systems

Founder: Allison Van Cura (StagePort) · Ask: 20–30 minute review of an 8-week pilot (Standard: $75k)

Py.rouette converts expert movement training into auditable decision-gating logic for noisy environments. In an 8-week pilot, we deliver a scoring model, event receipts, and a stability audit designed to reduce false triggers and improve control-loop reliability.

Problem

Operational decision systems frequently fail under noisy inputs: false triggers, oscillation, and inconsistent latency. Most gating logic is either too heuristic or too opaque for governance teams to inspect and trust.

Solution

  • Explicit states: named action and hold nodes.
  • Guards and permission gates: thresholded decision envelopes.
  • Risk-decay primitives: timed exits + hysteresis to prevent flip-flop behavior.
  • Receipts: per-decision audit packet (timestamp, sensor snapshot, rationale).

Why this matters to an operating fund

  • More stable control behavior under noise.
  • Lower-latency scoring designed for high-sampling streams.
  • Forensic, auditable event trails for compliance and debugging.
  • Transferable governance IP across trading and robotics workflows.
