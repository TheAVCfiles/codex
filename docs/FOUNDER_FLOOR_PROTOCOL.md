# Founder Floor Protocol

_Date captured: May 8, 2026 (ET)_

This document preserves the operating doctrine articulated in recent strategy logs.

## Core Thesis

The product is not "insight" alone; it is a translation layer that converts invisible human systems into institutional legibility.

## Primary Framing

- Product category: **Founder infrastructure for high-signal chaos**.
- Method: **The Floor Method / Founder Floor Protocol**.
- Outcome: **Institutional legibility**.
- Business posture: **Governance choreography**.

## Method Spine

1. **Floor**: establish legal/payment/proof primitives.
2. **Gap**: surface missing governance and boundary failures.
3. **Isolation**: correct one constrained subsystem at a time.
4. **Vertical Stack**: translate correction into operational artifacts.
5. **Resistance**: treat friction as diagnostic signal, not drama.

## Non-Negotiables

- Payment and scope terms speak before emotional interpretation.
- Work pauses when payment is overdue.
- No unpaid extraction.
- Preserve provenance and evidence trails.
- Keep naming, hierarchy, and routing coherent across all surfaces.
