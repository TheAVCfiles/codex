# StagePort Market Positioning Memo (May 11, 2026)

## Core Verdict

StagePort has crossed from conceptual framing into commercially coherent packaging. The market wedge is now clear: **proof infrastructure for movement studios**.

## Recommended Homepage Language

### Headline (safer)

**Turn rehearsals into credentials, ledgers, and review-ready reports.**

### Subhead

StagePort helps movement-based studios convert classes, rehearsals, scores, safety notes, and performances into portable proof: weekly StageCred reports, dancer portfolios, judging artifacts, and studio operating records.

### Risk-managed add-on line

Optional token and scholarship modules can fund travel, leadership, and student advancement.

## Why This Wedge Works

Studios need receipts. Parents need proof. Dancers need portable credentials. Owners need a system that reads as serious and institution-ready.

## Pricing Ladder (Current Strength)

- **StageCred Personal Ledger** — $19/month per dancer (recurring, low-friction)
- **Py.rouette Judging Package** — intro $249, from $900/event (event revenue)
- **StagePort Starter OS** — $297 one-time (owner install)

This ladder supports a realistic near-term path:

1. Student-ledger subscriptions
2. Event/judging economics
3. Studio OS installs
4. Later enterprise/license layer

## Strategic Messaging Anchors

- **Keep:** “Robotics kids get ecosystems. Dancers get ribbons.”
- **Follow with:** “StagePort turns performance labor into portable proof.”
- **Optional page anchor:** “Movement deserves math, ledgers, and exits too.”

## Compliance and Claims Guardrails

Do not overstate legal posture.

Preferred phrasing:

- “Title IX-supportive documentation logs”
- “incident documentation exports for school/studio review”

Avoid “Title IX-ready” unless legal review explicitly approves that claim.

## Product Doctrine to Keep

The 28-day operating rhythm is an asset, not marketing fluff.

- Week 1: Scan & Score
- Week 2: Ledger & Tokens
- Week 3–4: Patch & Release

This gives small studios an actionable cadence: what happens this week, what parents receive, and what improves next month.

## Commercial Architecture Snapshot

1. **Authority Layer:** Global AVC Systems institutional identity
2. **Services Layer:** Founder infrastructure sprints (bounded, paid, governance-first)
3. **Activation Layer:** Smaller install products with clear deliverables
4. **StagePort Product Layer:** Ledger + judging + starter OS
5. **Platform Layer:** Payment splits, records, safety logs, dashboards
6. **IP/Enterprise Layer:** Deeper governance and provenance systems

## Execution Priority

1. Keep homepage on proof + ledger + reports.
2. Position tokens as optional module.
3. Tighten claims language around compliance/safety.
4. Maintain recurring + event + install ladder.

## Working Thesis

Global AVC Systems converts high-friction human systems into institutionally legible operating infrastructure. StagePort applies that method to movement studios by turning rehearsals, scores, safety incidents, and performance labor into credentials, ledgers, reports, and funding loops.
