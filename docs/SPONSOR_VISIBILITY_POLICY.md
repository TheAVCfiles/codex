# SPONSOR_VISIBILITY_POLICY

## Purpose

Define how sponsors, partners, and infrastructure supporters are acknowledged without compromising governance posture.

## Visibility Rules

- Public acknowledgments must be accurate and current.
- “Supported by” language is reserved for verifiable relationships.
- Expired or inactive partner references should be archived promptly.

## Placement

Sponsor or partner references may appear in:

- partner stack pages,
- funding/support pages,
- project footers,
- investor or enterprise briefing docs.

## Disclosure Requirement

Any compensated relationship (cash, credits, referral payout, or equivalent benefit) must include clear disclosure nearby.

## Non-Transfer Rule

Sponsor visibility does not grant editorial control, ownership rights, or implied endorsement of all company activities.
