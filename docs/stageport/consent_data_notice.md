# StagePort Pilot Consent and Data Notice

**Audience:** parents/guardians and participating studios

## What data is used

During the pilot, StagePort may process:

- short training video clips
- instructor notes related to technique
- derived technical summaries (scores and comments)

StagePort does not perform identity verification.

## Why the data is used

Data is used only to:

- evaluate technical elements, and
- generate progress records for training purposes

## What is generated

The pilot generates:

- a technical summary report (PDF), and
- a numerical scorecard (CSV)

These outputs are training records and not competition results.

## How long data is kept

- Raw video is retained only as long as necessary to generate reports.
- After processing, raw media is deleted or returned per pilot terms.
- Generated reports may be retained by the studio for training records.

## Who can access the data

- the participating studio
- AVC Systems Studio for processing only

Data is not sold, publicly published, or shared with unrelated third parties.

## AI and reuse

Outputs may not be used to train AI systems or automated analysis tools unless separately authorized in writing.

## Consent

By participating in the pilot, the parent/guardian consents to:

- the limited processing described above, and
- generation of training records for the participating student

Consent may be withdrawn during the pilot by notifying the studio.
