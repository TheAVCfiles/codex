# FLOOR METHOD DOCTRINE

## Founder Legibility

We help founders become institutionally legible before opportunity arrives. Legibility is treated as infrastructure: records, decisions, controls, and provenance are organized to withstand diligence, speed, and scale.

## Governance Choreography

Governance is staged in sequence: identify authority, capture decision context, route approval, and archive the resulting artifact. The choreography preserves velocity while maintaining corporate clarity.

## Institutional Aesthetics

A founder system must read as coherent to external counterparties. Structure, naming, and records should signal discipline without performative complexity.

## Payment as Access Control

Payment is not a postscript; it is a control plane. Access to deeper implementation states is gated by payment confirmation and scope alignment.

## The Floor Method

The Floor Method converts conceptual energy into operational sequence:

1. Define the floor (facts, constraints, authority).
2. Set room state.
3. Route payment and permissions.
4. Execute the governed work.
5. Export evidence and closeout artifacts.

## Room States

Rooms are explicit operational states that set rules for engagement, evidence handling, and execution boundaries. State transitions are recorded to preserve continuity and reduce ambiguity.

## Founder Infrastructure Licensing

Founder infrastructure is licenseable IP. Frameworks, templates, and process artifacts are provisioned under bounded rights with clear implementation terms.

## Legitimacy Acceleration

Legitimacy is accelerated through repeatable records, transparent controls, and consistent execution proof. This shortens trust-building cycles with partners, investors, and clients.

## Anti-Extraction Doctrine

Work is structured to reduce unpaid extraction through clear scope boundaries, gated activation, and evidence-backed delivery.

## Graceful Degradation

When constraints appear (budget, timing, readiness), the system degrades to safer lower states rather than collapsing quality or governance.

## Human-in-the-loop Governance

Automation supports execution, but human judgment remains authoritative for edge cases, ethical decisions, and governance thresholds.
