# Hydrant Protocol

Date: 2026-05-08

## Purpose

The Hydrant Protocol defines how Global AVC Systems preserves operational safety infrastructure before, during, and after moments of founder panic, payment stress, extraction pressure, or room instability.

The protocol is inspired by a simple institutional principle:

> Hydrants must remain clear before the fire.

The point is not paranoia.

The point is preserving response capacity.

## Core Law

> A safety exit must remain available.
> A hydrant must remain unobstructed.

## Hydrant Definition

In The House and related systems, hydrants include:

- payment gates
- invoices
- pause authority
- room states
- access controls
- license notices
- agreements
- change orders
- evidence preservation
- operator exits
- platform compliance
- Stripe/payment policy alignment
- audit logs
- receipt systems
- provenance systems

These systems exist before conflict appears.

## Why Hydrants Matter

Most failures occur under pressure.

When panic increases:

- people reinterpret agreements
- founders seek optionality
- payment pressure increases
- emotional reasoning expands
- scope mutates
- extraction temptation rises
- operational memory degrades

Hydrants preserve the ability to respond coherently.

## Hydrant Obstruction

Hydrant obstruction occurs when a person attempts to bypass, block, weaken, or neutralize governance infrastructure designed to protect the room.

Examples:

- refusing payment while demanding continued work
- demanding deployment before activation
- attempting post-signature renegotiation without change order
- claiming induced performance has no value
- requesting refunds while retaining observed architecture
- attempting screenshot reconstruction of protected systems
- treating exits as permission for reuse
- bypassing agreed approval pathways
- blocking operator exits
- creating ambiguity around scope intentionally

## Safety Exit Abuse

A safe exit may not become an extraction route.

The system must preserve:

- reversibility
- humane exits
- non-coercive closure

while ALSO preserving:

- payment integrity
- IP integrity
- evidence continuity
- activation boundaries
- license boundaries

## Hydrant States

Suggested hydrant states:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| CLEAR | Infrastructure functioning normally |
| WATCH | Minor ambiguity or drift detected |
| REVIEW | Human review required |
| HARDENED | Gate tightened due to extraction/compliance risk |
| BLOCKED | Hydrant obstruction detected |
| RESOLVED | Risk resolved; room stabilized |
| CLOSED | Room closed and archived |

## Gate Hardening

Gate hardening may include:

- requiring written clarification
- limiting access
- narrowing exports
- pausing implementation
- requiring payment before continuation
- issuing license reminders
- reducing surface visibility
- requiring change order approval
- preserving additional evidence

Gate hardening is not punishment.

It is pressure containment.

## Panic Geometry

Under stress, humans expand behaviorally.

This may appear as:

- excessive messaging
- emotional reinterpretation
- urgency escalation
- contradictory requests
- shifting terms
- confusion around boundaries
- impulsive extraction attempts

The correct response is:

- slow down
- preserve exits
- preserve hydrants
- preserve receipts
- preserve room states
- reduce ambiguity
- avoid emotional escalation

## Institutional Alignment

Hydrant systems should align with:

- payment platform policies
- vendor agreements
- compliance obligations
- contract law
- evidence standards
- operational continuity practices

This reduces dependence on personal persuasion.

## Human-in-the-Loop Rule

Hydrant alerts should trigger review, not automatic accusation.

A human operator determines:

- confusion vs extraction
- panic vs manipulation
- creative drift vs obstruction
- misunderstanding vs bad faith

## Client-Safe Language

### WATCH

> Minor ambiguity detected. The room remains active while we clarify scope, payment, or access details.

### REVIEW

> The room has entered review. Work is temporarily slowed while payment, scope, or license conditions are verified.

### HARDENED

> Additional activation requirements are now in place due to unresolved payment, scope, or access ambiguity.

### BLOCKED

> Core governance infrastructure is currently obstructed. The room cannot proceed until the payment, license, or agreement state is clarified.

## Operator Rules

Do not personalize governance.

Do not emotionally negotiate against panic.

Do not weaken hydrants under pressure.

Do not remove exits.

Do not continue unpaid work to reduce discomfort.

Do preserve:

- receipts
- records
- room states
- evidence
- platform alignment
- operator exits

Hydrants create trust because the rules already exist before conflict begins.

## Final Compression

Hydrants are not built because every room is burning.

Hydrants are built because panic arrives faster than negotiation.

The room stays humane.

The exits stay open.

The hydrants stay clear.
