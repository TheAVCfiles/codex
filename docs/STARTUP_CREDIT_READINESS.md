# STARTUP_CREDIT_READINESS

## Goal

Prepare founders to apply for relevant startup credit programs with a legible operating profile.

## Readiness Checklist

- Legal entity details documented
- Company website and core narrative live
- Payment route active (at minimum one production checkout path)
- Product/repo surface publicly inspectable
- Basic analytics posture in place
- Founder contact and support routing published
- Governance and rights posture documented

## Application Evidence Pack

- One-page company summary
- Current stack diagram
- Use-case statement by provider category
- Forecasted consumption assumptions
- Security/privacy posture summary

## Sequencing Principle

Apply where immediate operating value is clear first (payments, deployment, analytics), then expand to adjacent partner programs.

## Warning

Credit awards are external decisions; this process improves readiness and clarity, not guaranteed acceptance.
