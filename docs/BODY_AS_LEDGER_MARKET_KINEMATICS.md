## The Floor Method

A foundational teaching method:

1. Put the body on the floor.
2. Let the spine feel what is touching and what is not.
3. Isolate the gap.
4. Activate the core without distorting the hips.
5. Release and feel the difference.
6. Stand the body up while preserving the relationship.

This becomes a founder infrastructure method:

1. Establish the floor: entity, payment, repo, offer, proof, governance.
2. Identify the gap: missing structure, unpaid work, unclear ownership, weak proof, scattered stack.
3. Isolate one correction at a time.
4. Translate the correction into the vertical operating stack.
5. Preserve the relationship under pressure.

The distance from the floor changes. The responsibility to alignment does not.
