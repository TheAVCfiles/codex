# CONTRIBUTING

## Contribution Policy

This repository contains controlled intellectual property, governance strategy, provenance structures, and system architecture associated with Allison Van Cura / TheAVCfiles.

Contributions are welcome only when they preserve authorship, provenance, and licensing boundaries.

## No Implied Rights

Submitting, reviewing, commenting on, forking, cloning, or otherwise interacting with this repository does not grant any right to commercially reuse, repackage, train on, publish, sell, sublicense, or extract its contents.

## Contributor Warranty

By submitting a contribution, you represent that:

- you have the right to submit the contribution
- the contribution does not knowingly infringe another party's rights
- the contribution does not add confidential third-party material without authorization
- the contribution can be governed under this repository's existing IP posture

## Contribution License

Unless a separate written agreement states otherwise, any accepted contribution is provided to Allison Van Cura / TheAVCfiles under a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, modify, govern, archive, commercialize, sublicense, and incorporate the contribution into MEMOS, StagePort, DeCrypt the Girl, AVC Systems, and related works.

This does not transfer ownership of the contributor's pre-existing independent IP. It does allow the repository owner to safely operate and commercialize accepted contributions as part of the governed system.

## Provenance Requirement

Every substantial contribution should identify:

- source files changed
- reason for change
- whether external source material was used
- whether AI tools were used
- whether any licensing restrictions apply

## AI-Generated Contributions

AI-assisted contributions must be disclosed in the pull request or commit message when material.

Do not submit AI-generated material copied from proprietary, private, confidential, or third-party restricted sources.

## No Extraction Rule

Do not use contribution access as a way to extract, clone, repackage, or reverse-engineer the system for unpaid commercial use.

The operating principle remains:

> Clean front door, preserved roots. Paid access before extraction.

## Governance Review

Maintainers may reject, rewrite, archive, or hold contributions when they create provenance risk, safety risk, licensing ambiguity, commercial ambiguity, or strategic dilution.
