# SPONSORSHIP

## Sponsorship Philosophy

Strategic visibility is part of the business model.

Public contribution should:

- increase trust
- increase authority
- create gratitude
- create discoverability
- attract aligned operators
- generate inbound demand
- strengthen category ownership

without giving away core infrastructure.

## Public Contribution Strategy

Contribute publicly in ways that are:

- useful
- repeatable
- visible
- low-cost to produce
- high-value to others
- non-destructive to core IP

Examples:

- governance templates
- repo hardening guides
- onboarding checklists
- provenance structures
- Stripe gating examples
- deployment fixes
- lightweight utilities
- educational threads
- founder workflows

## The Ballet Translation

Old model:

school exposure -> trusted teacher -> studio conversion -> birthday party premium -> long-term loyalty

Current model:

open-source visibility -> trusted systems architect -> paid room access -> intensive -> implementation/license

The mechanism is the same.

Visibility creates familiarity.
Familiarity creates trust.
Trust creates inevitability.

## Sponsorship Paths

Potential support mechanisms:

- direct Stripe support
- subscriptions
- sponsor tiers
- founder memberships
- public tool sponsorship
- educational sponsorship
- ecosystem partnerships
- startup ecosystem visibility
- accelerator exposure
- public-good tooling support

## Visibility Rules

Do publicly:

- solvable problems
- visible infrastructure improvements
- founder education
- governance frameworks
- workflow simplification
- elegant implementation patterns

Do not publicly expose:

- canonical IP cores
- private archive systems
- full transformation pipelines
- sensitive provenance structures
- enterprise implementation details
- private strategic doctrine

## Strategic Objective

The goal is not merely attention.

The goal is:

- category ownership
- trust accumulation
- inbound demand
- pricing power
- institutional legitimacy
- enterprise leverage

## Emotional Positioning

People should feel:

> "This person understands systems at a level I trust."

before they ever encounter a sales process.

## Result

The strongest marketing outcome is:

> paying feels natural because trust already exists before the transaction.
