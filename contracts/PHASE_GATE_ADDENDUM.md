# Phase Gate Addendum

Date: 2026-05-08

This Phase Gate Addendum supplements the primary agreement between Provider (Global AVC Systems, Inc.) and Client.

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this addendum is to define:

- phase activation logic
- payment gates
- induced performance boundaries
- access boundaries
- licensing limits
- change-order procedures
- pause and exit states

## 2. Phase Activation

Each phase activates only after:

1. required payment is received,
2. required agreement state is complete,
3. required scope is approved,
4. required access boundaries are accepted.

Partial payment does not automatically activate future phases.

## 3. Deposit Activation

A deposit activates only the deposit-defined work window.

Provider retains the right to:

- limit access,
- limit deployment,
- limit exports,
- limit implementation,
- hold future work,

until the applicable phase payment is complete.

## 4. Induced Performance

Client acknowledges that performance may be induced through:

- meetings,
- reviews,
- strategic direction,
- approvals,
- demos,
- architecture discussion,
- implementation planning,
- feedback,
- mockups,
- room setup,
- governance review,
- payment routing,
- startup infrastructure sequencing,
- founder-system configuration.

Induced performance constitutes professional labor even where:

- final deployment has not occurred,
- production access has not been granted,
- integration has not been completed,
- exports remain gated,
- implementation remains incomplete pending payment.

## 5. Observation Does Not Transfer Ownership

Client acknowledges that:

- demos,
- screenshots,
- review access,
- mockups,
- architecture visibility,
- meetings,
- presentations,
- staged systems,
- partial exports,

DO NOT transfer ownership of:

- frameworks,
- methodologies,
- governance systems,
- workflows,
- room architecture,
- proof systems,
- payment systems,
- operational doctrine,
- implementation patterns,
- underlying intellectual property.

## 6. No Unauthorized Reuse

Without explicit written license permission, Client may not:

- reverse engineer,
- clone,
- reproduce,
- sublicense,
- commercialize,
- train AI systems on,
- distribute,
- recreate from screenshots,
- recreate from observation,
- recreate from exports,

any Provider-owned protected systems.

## 7. Change Orders

Post-signature modifications require a written change order.

Change orders may affect:

- price,
- timeline,
- scope,
- access,
- deliverables,
- licensing,
- support,
- implementation sequence.

Provider is not required to honor verbal or informal scope changes.

## 8. Pause Rights

Provider may pause the room (FERMATA state) when:

- payment is overdue,
- scope becomes unclear,
- extraction risk appears,
- access boundaries are violated,
- trust materially degrades,
- compliance review is required,
- Client attempts unauthorized reuse,
- change-order disputes remain unresolved.

While paused:

- work may stop,
- access may narrow,
- implementation may halt,
- exports may remain gated.

## 9. Exit Rights

Client retains the right to exit the engagement.

However:

- exit does not erase induced performance,
- exit does not transfer ownership,
- exit does not authorize reuse,
- exit does not invalidate unpaid labor,
- exit does not grant derivative rights.

Safe exits are preserved.

Extraction routes are not authorized.

## 10. Evidence Preservation

Provider may preserve:

- invoices,
- receipts,
- agreements,
- room states,
- access records,
- exports,
- changelogs,
- communications,
- payment records,

for business, legal, compliance, and operational continuity purposes.

## 11. Room States

Canonical room states include:

- GLISSADE (prep)
- FERMATA (pause/review)
- JETÉ (active)
- CODA (close)
- RESET (archive/next phase)

Client acknowledges these are operational governance states, not emotional judgments.

## 12. No Implied Partnership or Equity

Unless expressly stated in writing:

- no equity is transferred,
- no partnership is formed,
- no cofounder relationship is implied,
- no ownership rights are granted.

Provider supplies governed infrastructure and related services under license and contract.

## 13. Final Compression

The room may stay humane without allowing unpaid extraction.

Activation requires quorum.

Payment gates remain enforceable.

The operating record survives panic.
