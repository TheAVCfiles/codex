# AVC Financial Operating System (v1.0)

## Purpose

Build a governance-first company into a compounding financial system with four integrated layers:

1. Entity + Tax Layer
2. Revenue Layer
3. Credit Layer
4. Asset Layer

---

## 1) Entity + Tax Layer (Legal Spine)

### Entity architecture

- **Parent:** `Global AVC Systems, Inc.` (Delaware C-Corp)
  - Holds all IP, brand, and system logic.
- **Operating units:**
  - AVC Systems Studio (services / installs)
  - Intuition Labs R+D (research / publication)
- Implement operating units as DBAs or subsidiaries; route all commercial value to parent ownership.

### Core governance rules

- Assign all relevant IP into parent entity.
- Keep licensing boundaries explicit (internal-use, non-transfer, commercial upgrades).
- Preserve founder control as default cap table posture.

### Tax posture (year 1)

- Operate as C-Corp for reinvestment-focused growth.
- Prioritize business reinvestment and clean accounting over personal extraction.
- Maintain defensible records for:
  - product/system development
  - cloud and infrastructure
  - legal and contracting
  - research and publication support

### Tax operations checklist

- Open dedicated business bank + bookkeeping stack.
- Track every expense category monthly.
- Store contracts, scopes, invoices, and receipts in one place.
- Engage CPA for R&D treatment, startup costs, and entity election timing.

## 2) Revenue Layer (Cash Engine)

### Product framing

Do not position as “freelance services.”
Position as **governed infrastructure installs + licensing rights**.

### Offer stack

- **Tier 1: Governance Install** — $2.5K–$5K
  - Outcome: fundable structure + risk map + operating boundaries.
- **Tier 2: Deployment License / Build** — $7.5K–$15K
  - Outcome: working system in production-ready form.
- **Tier 3: Institutional Engagement** — $25K–$100K+
  - Outcome: audit-ready or enterprise-grade operating system.
- **Tier 4: Licensing**
  - Per organization: $10K–$50K/year
  - White-label / government / studio: $50K–$250K+

### Commercial rules

- Never start delivery before payment.
- Keep scope fixed by phase.
- Move clients from Phase I to Phase II within 7–14 days.
- Anchor all expansion in license terms, not ad hoc work.

---

## 3) Credit Layer (Leverage Engine)

### Phase timeline

#### Weeks 1–2

- Open operating accounts (bank + payments).
- Apply for startup-focused corporate cards.
- Target initial usable access: **$10K–$50K**.

#### Months 1–2

- Add vendor trade lines and recurring paid accounts.
- Maintain early-payment discipline.
- Apply for additional business cards.
- Target total access: **$50K–$150K**.

#### Months 3–6

- Apply for larger revolving facilities / line of credit.
- Increase limits with stable utilization and on-time history.
- Target usable access: **$150K–$300K**.

### Credit rules

- Use credit for growth assets only: infrastructure, delivery, client acquisition, and production.
- Do not use business credit for lifestyle spending.
- Protect utilization and payment history as strategic assets.

---

## 4) Asset Layer (Long-Term Wealth)

### Core assets

1. **IP portfolio**
   - Governance frameworks, architecture methods, branded systems.
2. **Research authority**
   - DOI/published work used for credibility and enterprise trust.
3. **Contract architecture**
   - Internal-use boundaries, anti-replication controls, licensing escalation.

### Asset protection rules

- Keep all templates and methods under controlled license terms.
- Enforce non-transfer and derivative-use restrictions.
- Separate “client implementation rights” from “platform/system ownership.”

---

## Money Loop (Compounding Cycle)

1. Close a deployment engagement ($7.5K–$15K).
2. Invest a controlled amount into delivery/infrastructure.
3. Capture deductible operating expenses through clean books.
4. Preserve margin.
5. Strengthen credit profile.
6. Reinvest leverage into next close.

Result: increasing authority, stronger capital access, and higher-quality deal flow.
