# Prati Lineage Research Framework

_Last consolidated: 2026-03-15 22:24 ET_

## Purpose

This document separates **verifiable genealogy workflow** from **mythic/narrative interpretation** for the Prati/Van Cura lineage thread.

---

## 1) Core Record Classes (Genealogy Gold Standard)

### Vital records

- Birth
- Marriage
- Death

Use these to establish parent-child links.

### Census and population records

- U.S. Census
- Italian population registers

Use these to place people in time and location.

### Church records

Before civil registration, churches tracked:

- baptisms
- marriages
- burials

In Italian research these are often foundational.

### Probate records

Track:

- heirs
- property
- family relationships

---

## 2) Primary Italian and U.S. Research Tools

### Antenati Portal

- https://antenati.cultura.gov.it
- Civil records, often 1800s onward:
  - birth acts
  - marriage acts
  - death acts

### FamilySearch

- https://familysearch.org
- Useful for:
  - Italian parish registers
  - immigration records
  - U.S. census
  - military records

### Archivio di Stato (priority provinces)

- Turin
- Alessandria

Potential holdings:

- military service files
- noble registers
- property archives

## 3) Noble Title Verification (Historical, Not Legal)

Potential evidence repositories:

- _Libro d’Oro della Nobiltà Italiana_
- Consulta Araldica archives
- Savoy state registers

Important legal context: post-1948 Italian Republic abolished legal recognition of nobility; modern verification is historical.

---

## 4) High-Value Military Record Target

If Carabinieri officer service is claimed, priority document:

### Stato di Servizio

Expected fields:

- birth date
- parents
- military career timeline
- promotions
- children
- retirement status

This is one of the strongest single-document biographical sources.

---

## 5) U.S. Transition Records

To map identity transition from Italy to the U.S., prioritize:

- passenger lists
- naturalization records
- city directories
- draft registrations
- marriage certificates
