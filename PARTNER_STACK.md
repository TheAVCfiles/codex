# PARTNER_STACK

## Purpose

The Partner Stack is the public-facing infrastructure map for Global AVC Systems. It exists to make founder legibility practical: tools, credits, and integrations are organized as an operating sequence instead of random recommendations.

## Core Stack Categories

1. **Payments & Finance**: Stripe, Ramp
2. **Cloud & Deployment**: Google Cloud, Vercel
3. **Source Control & Startup Programs**: GitHub, GitHub for Startups
4. **Product Analytics & Messaging**: PostHog, Intercom
5. **Streaming/Data Backbone**: Confluent Cloud

## Founder Use Pattern

- Validate legal entity and governance posture.
- Activate payment surface and receipt trail.
- Provision cloud/runtime stack.
- Instrument product analytics and lifecycle messaging.
- Document proofs, eligibility, and partner outcomes.

## Disclosure Standard

Some links in this stack may be partner, referral, or affiliate links. Global AVC Systems may receive credits, discounts, or compensation when eligible users enroll.

## Boundary

Partner tools are acceleration channels, not guarantees. Credit approval and partner eligibility remain subject to each provider’s rules.
