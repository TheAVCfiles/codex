Slide 1 · Problem / Solution / Value

Decision systems break under noisy inputs. Teams pay for reduced false triggers, stable gating, and auditable events.

Problem

  • False triggers and oscillation in live loops.
  • Latency spikes under stress.
  • Action logic that is hard to audit and govern.

Py.rouette solution

  • Finite-state scoring layer from expert embodied training.
  • Explicit states, guards, and permission gates.
  • Risk-decay + hysteresis to prevent flip-flop behavior.
  • Per-decision receipts for forensic traceability.

Why it matters

  • Operational stability under noisy signals.
  • Low-latency scoring for high-frequency sensor streams.
  • Governance-ready audit trails and transferable control IP.
