## Working Thesis

Global AVC Systems appears to be building governance-native, meaning-preserving infrastructure rather than a conventional AI wrapper. The strongest evidence path is not rhetorical framing; it is repository activity plus payment, release, and policy artifacts.

### 2) Governance + provenance artifacts

- JSON-LD, `llms.txt`, `ai-rights.txt`, and canonical/proof routes support machine-readable attribution and indexing boundaries.
- MEMOS-style promotion gates (source paths, commit hash, score, promotion reason, destination, safety visibility) indicate governed archive flow.
- Security/provenance release posture (headers, signed receipt continuity, issuer fingerprint continuity) suggests verification-first discipline.

### 3) Commercial + operations artifacts

- Founder service ladder appears structured:
  - entry activation offer,
  - higher-touch infrastructure sprint,
  - later licensing/buildout extensions.
- Payment rails and payout references suggest real transactions with mixed states (succeeded/blocked/failed), increasing credibility vs mock environments.
- Partner stack and startup-credit readiness point to practical execution sequencing.
