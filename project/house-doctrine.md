# The House Doctrine (Operating Draft)

## Core Positioning

Global AVC Systems converts founder chaos into governed, fundable, cloud-ready infrastructure.

- **Book the room.** A scoped engagement container.
- **Clear the gate.** Payment/readiness checkpoint.
- **Receive the key.** State-based license/access rights.
- **Stay on the rail.** Ongoing support and maintenance pathway.

## Canonical Service Ladder

1. **Governance Install — $2,500**  
   Record cleanup, authority posture, boundary map, funder legibility.
2. **CTO Runway Sprint — $2,000 beta (target $3,500–$5,000)**  
   7-day bounded readiness conversion sprint.
3. **Phase II Activation — $15,000**  
   $7,500 entry + $7,500 completion.
4. **Commercial Go-Live Retainer — $1,500/mo × 16 months minimum**  
   Governance, maintenance, implementation oversight.

**Minimum account path (Beta):** $43,500 before licensing upside.

## Scope Boundary Doctrine

- Completed architecture/governance work remains compensable regardless of later funding delays.
- No production-risk work begins without cleared activation funds.
- New modules, extra brands, emergency rebuilds, legal documentation, and heavy custom development are separately scoped.
- Urgency does not expand scope.

## Capacity Is Inventory Policy

Operator attention is inventory and can be:

- included in scope,
- billed as room service,
- logged as incidental,
- or declined.

Urgency, emotional pressure, and disorganization do not convert provider capacity into unpaid runway.

## Compliance-Gated Activation (Phase II)

Before compliance-sensitive go-live pathways, require checklist confirmation for:

- consent model and records,
- opt-in/opt-out handling,
- privacy policy and data purpose,
- retention period,
- subprocessor list,
- controller/processor role,
- region-sensitive handling (e.g., UK/EU contexts).

## Public-Safe Messaging

- “Cloud architecture begins before deployment.”
- “Runway is not revenue. Governance converts runway into fundable execution.”
- “A blueprint is not free because financing collapses.”
